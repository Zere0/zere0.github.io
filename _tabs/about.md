Hi, I'm Jorge Cerezo, also known as zere. I'm an independent security researcher from Madrid, focused on web application security, bug bounty, and pentesting.
{: .about-lead}

I'm especially interested in vulnerabilities that require understanding how a product really works. Business logic flaws, unexpected interactions between features, and small issues that become much more serious when combined. Since 2022, I've reported vulnerabilities affecting more than 400 companies, including Amazon, Salesforce, Google, Epic Games, PayPal, GitHub, TikTok, and Inditex.

Live hacking events have been an important part of my journey. I've participated in more than 10 events around the world and achieved several Top 10 finishes, including second place at H1-361 in Bali and Best Hacker at H1-353 in Cork. Some of my reports have been selected for Show & Tell presentations and received bonuses for their critical impact and creativity. I've also taken part in several private security research events with Amazon and AWS.

Together with Team Spain, I won the HackerOne Ambassadors World Cup twice. Beyond competitions and bug bounty programs, I also work on private pentesting engagements through HackerOne and Intigriti.

I hold a degree in Computer Engineering from *Universidad Politécnica de Madrid*. [My final thesis](https://oa.upm.es/90101/1/TFG_JORGE_CEREZO_DACOSTA.pdf){:target="_blank" title="PDF, in Spanish"} was based on [a security review of several university applications]({% post_url 2025-10-06-university-web-audit %}), where I found and helped remediate critical vulnerabilities affecting systems used by hundreds of students and faculty members.

I enjoy sharing what I learn through [technical write-ups]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) on this site and talks at events such as RootedCON, INCIBE Tardes Hacker, and Bug Summit.
